# Initial Concept of Operations (CONOPS) — Draft
**Date**: 2026-07-18

## High-Level Mission Phases
1. **Payload Integration** (dedicated or rideshare)
2. **Launch Campaign** (transport, integration, countdown)
3. **Ascent & Separation**
4. **Booster Recovery**
   - Flip maneuver, reentry, landing burn
   - **Alternative explored**: Net capture on drone ship (inspired by 2026 Long March 10B success)
5. **Upper Stage Operations** (payload deployment, deorbit if needed)
6. **Post-Flight**
   - Booster inspection/refurb
   - Data review
   - Quick turnaround

## Key Operational Requirements (to be quantified)
- Launch site options: Coastal (Hainan-style) + sea recovery zones
- Weather constraints: Standard (wind, lightning)
- Ground ops: Minimal infrastructure for rapid reuse
- Recovery assets: Drone ship or platform (or net system)

## Responsive Launch Variant
- Pre-positioned booster + payload "on alert"
- 14–30 day call-up

**Novel Idea**: Hybrid recovery — propulsive + net for reduced propellant penalty on high-performance missions.
