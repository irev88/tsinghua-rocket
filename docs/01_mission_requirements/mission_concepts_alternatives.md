# Alternative Mission Concepts (Day 1 Brainstorm)
**Date**: 2026-07-18 | **Grounded in Research**

## Concept A: "Green Constellation Enabler" (Preferred Novel Path)
- **Mission**: Optimized for lowest lifecycle emissions while delivering 1.2–2 t to LEO/SSO for sustainable megaconstellations.
- **Key Requirements**: Methalox (or bio-methane), GRI ≥ 1.2× Falcon 9 equivalent, 15+ reuses.
- **Recovery**: Evaluate net capture (LM-10B 2026) to reduce landing mass penalty.
- **Feasibility**: High — aligns with Zhuque-3 / New Glenn propellant shift.

## Concept B: "Rapid Response Dual-Use"
- **Mission**: 14-day surge capability for defense + commercial constellation replenishment.
- **Differentiator**: "Launch-on-alert" infrastructure + minimal refurb.
- **Trade-off**: Higher development cost for rapid ops.

## Concept C: "Falcon 9 Lite with Chinese Twist"
- Conservative: Direct analog to Pallas-1 / Hyperbola-3 class but with strong sustainability scoring and net-capture option.
- **Pros**: Lower risk.
- **Cons**: Less novel.

**Recommendation**: Proceed with A as primary, evaluate B/C in trades (Day 6–7).
