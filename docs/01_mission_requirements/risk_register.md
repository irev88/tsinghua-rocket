# Initial Risk Register (Day 1)
**Version**: 0.1 | **Date**: 2026-07-18

| ID   | Risk Description                                      | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation / Notes (Research-backed) | Owner |
|------|-------------------------------------------------------|------------|--------|--------------------------------------|-------|
| R01  | Recovery failure reduces effective payload too much  | Medium    | High  | Trade propulsive legs vs net-capture (LM-10B 2026 demonstrated net reduces leg mass). Baseline RTLS vs downrange. | Structures/Recovery |
| R02  | New Chinese vehicles achieve lower cost/kg faster than expected | Medium    | Medium | Monitor Pallas-1, Zhuque-3, Hyperbola-3 cadence. Our concept must differentiate on sustainability or responsiveness. | Systems |
| R03  | Methalox boil-off impacts rapid turnaround            | Medium    | Medium | Chinese LM-12A and Zhuque-3 chose methalox for reusability despite boil-off. Mitigate with insulation or quick-fill. | Propulsion |
| R04  | Regulatory approval for commercial reusable delayed  | Low       | High  | Reference FAA commercial path + Chinese commercial licensing (Hainan/Wenchang). Build traceability early. | Program |
| R05  | Sustainability requirements conflict with performance | Medium    | Medium | Include GRI as FoM. Reusability itself reduces manufacturing emissions (LCA papers). | Systems |
| R06  | Payload market shifts away from dedicated small launches | Low-Medium | Medium | Support both dedicated + rideshare (common in Falcon 9/Neutron). | Market |
| R07  | First-stage structural mass higher than estimated    | High      | High  | Use 2026 data: Falcon ~ structural coeff trends; Chinese stainless designs. Apply margins + sensitivity. | Structures |

**Notes**: Risks updated from real 2025-2026 developments (e.g., net recovery success, multiple Chinese recovery attempts with mixed results).
