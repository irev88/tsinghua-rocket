# Stakeholder Analysis (Day 1 Draft)
**Date**: 2026-07-18

## Primary Stakeholders
1. **Commercial Constellation Operators** (e.g., analogs to Starlink, Qianfan/Thousand Sails, Kuiper)
   - Needs: High cadence, low cost/kg, dedicated or flexible orbits, rapid replenishment.
   - Influence: High (drives volume).

2. **Government / National Space Agencies** (CASC, NASA, ESA analogs; defense)
   - Needs: Reliability, responsive launch, sovereignty, national security payloads.
   - Influence: High (contracts, regulation).

3. **Scientific / University Payload Providers**
   - Needs: Affordable dedicated access, flexible scheduling, lower reliability threshold sometimes.
   - Influence: Medium (niche but drives innovation).

4. **Launch Service Providers / Our Team**
   - Needs: Profitable operations, reusability economics, regulatory approval.

5. **Regulators & Environmental Bodies**
   - Needs: Safety, orbital debris compliance (e.g., 5-year deorbit), emissions reporting (emerging).

## Secondary
- Insurance providers (reusability reduces premiums long-term)
- Manufacturing supply chain (3D printing, composites, methane engines)
- End users of data (EO, comms, defense)

## Key Conflicts / Trades
- Cost vs Reliability vs Sustainability
- Responsiveness (rapid launch) vs Refurb time
- Payload performance vs Recovery propellant budget

Stakeholder needs → L0 objectives → L1/L2 requirements (see mission_requirements.md)
