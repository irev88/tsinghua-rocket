**Key Insights from Comparison**:
- Chinese vehicles clustering around 8–18 t reusable class (Falcon 9 competitive).
- Propellant shift to methalox for reusability (cleaner, easier maintenance).
- Recovery innovation: China demonstrated **net capture** (LM-10B 2026) — reduces structural mass vs legs.
- Fairing sizes: 4.2–7 m common.
- Cost pressure: Reusability essential for megaconstellation economics.

**Estimations** (clearly labeled):
- Long March 10B LEO ~16 t: Derived from "approximately Falcon 9 class" reporting.
- Cost targets for new Chinese: Not publicly detailed; assumed competitive with Falcon 9 for domestic contracts.
