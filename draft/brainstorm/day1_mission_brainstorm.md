# Day 1 Brainstorm: Mission Definition Ideas (Novel + Realistic)
**Date**: 2026-07-18 | **Based on Research** (2025-2026 data)

## 1. Novel Mission Profiles
- **"Green Constellation Enabler" Mission**: Primary objective is minimizing lifecycle CO2e per kg delivered to support sustainable mega-constellations (Qianfan analogs). Requirement: GRI (Green Reusability Index) ≥ baseline Falcon 9 equivalent. Uses methalox + high reuse + bio-methane option.
  - Feasibility: Supported by emerging LCA work. Chinese vehicles already shifting to methalox.
  - Trade: Slightly higher propellant mass vs RP-1 for cleaner burn.

- **Responsive + Dual-Use (Civil + Defense)**: 14-day call-up for one class of payload + standard commercial. Include "surge capacity" requirement (2 launches in 10 days).
  - Realistic: Matches DoD "tactically responsive space" and Chinese constellation replenishment urgency.

- **Ocean-Platform Net-Capture Primary**: Make net capture (demonstrated by Long March 10B July 2026) the baseline recovery instead of legs. Saves ~mass on landing gear.
  - Novel angle for conceptual vehicle: Reduces dry mass penalty of reusability.
  - Risk: Requires dedicated recovery ship infrastructure (already procured by iSpace).

## 2. Requirements Innovations
- **Tiered Reusability**: 
  - Threshold: 10 flights
  - Goal: 20+ flights with "certified refurb" after every 5.
  - Stretch (novel): Design for "minimal inspection" reuse after flight 5 (inspired by Falcon rapid reuse).

- **Sustainability as Hard Requirement** (not just nice-to-have):
  - L1-E03: Propellant production must allow carbon-negative or offset pathway within 5 years of ops.
  - Include "debris mitigation score" (post-mission disposal compliance built-in).

- **Multi-Plane Constellation Optimizer**: Requirement that vehicle can support both SSO and inclined orbits with minimal performance loss (phasing efficiency).

## 3. Stakeholder-Driven Requirements
- For Chinese commercial context: Include "domestic manufacturing >70%" or "compatible with Hainan commercial spaceport" as constraint (realistic given rapid Chinese ecosystem).
- University payload: Low-cost "smallsat rideshare kit" interface requirement.

## 4. Risk-Informed Requirements
- From risk register: Add explicit "recovery propellant budget margin ≥ 15%" to protect payload.
- "Alternative recovery architecture evaluation" as a formal L1 requirement (propulsive vs net).

## 5. Creative but Grounded Ideas to Evaluate Later
- "Refurb-as-a-Service" model: Booster returns to customer site for refurb (reduces logistics emissions).
- "Payload-adaptive fairing": Modular fairing sections that can be reconfigured quickly.
- Tie reusability number directly to environmental KPI (more flights = better GRI).

**Evaluation Criteria for Inclusion**:
- Backed by at least one real 2025-2026 vehicle/example
- Quantifiable
- Creates interesting trade space for Days 2-7
