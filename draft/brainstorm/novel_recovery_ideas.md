# Brainstorm: Novel / Niche Recovery Concepts
**Date**: 2026-07-18  
**Status**: Raw ideas — not yet evaluated  
**Category**: Recovery & Reusability (Day 6 focus)

## 1. Hybrid "Drone-Assisted Mid-Air Capture + Retro"
- Use a parafoil or steerable parachute for initial deceleration.
- Deploy a small drone (or swarm) from the booster or ground that performs precision docking/capture in mid-air.
- Reduces terminal velocity requirements → less propellant for landing burn.
- Potential for ocean or remote land recovery without heavy legs.

**Pros**: Lower landing propellant budget, softer landing loads.
**Cons**: Adds complexity, drone reliability in high winds.
**Novel angle**: Treat the drone as a "reusable tether" system.

## 2. Bio-Inspired Variable Geometry + Morphing Surfaces
- Inspired by bird wing morphing or insect elytra.
- Grid fins or control surfaces that change shape (via shape-memory alloys or inflatable structures) between ascent (low drag) and descent (high drag + steering).
- Could enable "perching" style recovery on prepared pads.

## 3. Ocean Platform with Active Wave Compensation + Magnetic Capture
- Floating platform with heave compensation + electromagnetic "soft dock" (inspired by space docking but scaled down).
- Eliminates need for legs entirely; vehicle hovers briefly and is captured.

## 4. "Smart" Propellant Slosh Damping + ML Landing Controller
- Use reinforcement learning (trained in sim) for real-time landing control that accounts for slosh, wind gusts, and structural flex.
- Onboard tiny model (edge AI) that was co-trained with the design.

## 5. Circular Recovery Hardware
- Design landing legs and grid fins to be modular and easily remanufactured.
- Use additive manufacturing so that damaged components can be reprinted from recycled material on-site.

**Next step idea**: Run a very simple trade study (mass penalty vs reliability) on 3 of these vs classic Falcon-style propulsive landing.
